WHAT WE DO
Q: What does Weblyft Design do?
A: Weblyft Design is a web design and development agency based in New Delhi, India. We build custom websites, landing pages, and web applications for local businesses. Every site is custom-designed — no templates — and pricing is transparent.

Q: What kind of businesses do you work with?
A: We specialize in local businesses. Past and current projects include roofing companies, dental studios, landscaping businesses, salons, fitness gyms, restaurants, and tattoo studios.

Q: Do you only make full websites, or also single pages and web apps?
A: All three. We build custom websites, single landing pages, and web applications depending on what your business needs.

Q: Do you use templates or WordPress themes?
A: No templates. Every project is custom-designed from scratch to match your brand, so your site does not look like anyone else's.

SERVICES & PRICING
Q: How much does a website cost?
A: There are three packages: Starter at $899 (single-page site), Professional at $1,799 (full 5-page business website), and Premium at $2,799 (Professional plus a built-in blog system). All pricing is transparent.

Q: What is included in the Starter package ($899)?
A: A single-page website — ideal for new businesses or freelancers that need an online presence fast. It includes a hero section with call-to-action, about section, services showcase, project gallery, contact form with Google Apps Script integration, mobile-responsive design, and free hosting setup.

Q: What is included in the Professional package ($1,799)?
A: A full 5-page business website: homepage, about page, services page, gallery, and contact page. It adds SEO optimization, Google Analytics integration, Schema.org structured data for rich search results, contact form with email notifications, mobile-responsive design, free hosting setup, and delivery in 10-14 business days.

Q: What is included in the Premium package ($2,799)?
A: Everything in Professional, plus a built-in blog system with a blog listing page, individual blog post pages, blog category tagging, SEO-optimized blog posts, newsletter signup integration, and priority support.

Q: Which package is right for a brand-new business?
A: The Starter package is designed for new businesses or freelancers that just need a simple, professional online presence quickly.

Q: Which package is right if I want to grow my traffic with content?
A: The Premium package — its built-in blog lets you publish regular posts, which improves search visibility. Clients using the blog report strong growth in organic traffic.

Q: Do you include SEO in the price?
A: SEO optimization is included in the Professional package and above. The Starter package does not list SEO as an included feature.

Q: Is hosting included?
A: Free hosting setup is included with the Starter and Professional packages. If you need specifics about ongoing hosting costs, ask via the contact form.
